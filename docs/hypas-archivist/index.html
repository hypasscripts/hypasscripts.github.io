CORE CAPABILITIES

Designed for meaningful RedM gameplay.

Gameplay systems and administrative controls described in the current official product listing.

01 / DISCOVERY

Search the frontier

Visit 12 configurable discovery locations, find unidentified relics and uncover items of different rarity and condition.

02 / APPRAISAL

Give each find a history

Take discoveries for appraisal to learn their identity and potential value, then choose whether to sell, keep or donate them.

03 / MUSEUM

Build a shared collection

Contribute artefacts to eight themed collections, track collective progress and work towards collection milestones.

04 / INVESTIGATION

Follow historical mysteries

Three multi-stage investigations take players through clues, locations and recovered evidence.

05 / PERSONAL PROGRESS

Preserve your discoveries

Track discoveries in a catalogue and keep favourite pieces in a personal eight-slot display cabinet.

06 / REPEATABLE ACTIVITY

Curator commissions

Rotating weekly museum requests offer additional objectives and reputation progression.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Know what you're installing.

Can players keep what they discover?

Yes. After appraisal, players can choose to retain discoveries rather than donate or sell them.

Does it include investigation gameplay?

Yes. Three multi-stage investigations provide historical mysteries beyond the core search loop.

Is the full script public on GitHub?

No. The commercial gameplay code is distributed through Tebex using Cfx Asset Escrow; editable configuration remains available to customers.

How do I get support?

Join The Lobby Discord for product guidance and support. Check the requirements and included installation instructions before deploying.
