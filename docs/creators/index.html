How it works

01 / APPLY

Tell us about your content

Share your YouTube, TikTok, Twitch or community channels through a Discord ticket. We're interested in engaged audiences and genuine RedM content, not just follower counts.

02 / AGREE

Receive a personal code

If we're a good match, we'll agree the promotional arrangement, commission, applicable products and any buyer discount. Your individual code is issued through Tebex.

03 / EARN

Recommend what you've tested

Share your code with your audience. Eligible purchases using the code are tracked through Tebex, with creator earnings allocated to a Tebex Wallet.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a public creator code?

No. Accepted partners receive their own code so referrals can be tracked fairly and attributed to the correct creator.

How are earnings paid?

Creator Codes are processed through Tebex. Approved creators need a Tebex Wallet to receive allocations and withdrawals, subject to Tebex terms and settlement periods.

Does everyone receive the same rate?

Rates, eligible products, buyer discounts and any deliverables are discussed and agreed before a partnership begins.

Do I need a huge following?

No fixed audience threshold is advertised. Relevant content, genuine engagement and a good fit with Hypas matter more than raw follower counts.

Do I need to purchase scripts first?

Not to enquire. Any testing access or sample arrangements are discussed individually and are not guaranteed.

Are paid or affiliate videos disclosed?

Yes. Creators should clearly disclose affiliate relationships, commissions or sponsorships where required by the applicable platform and advertising rules.
