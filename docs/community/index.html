Before you join

A few straight answers.

Do I need to buy a Hypas script?

No. The Lobby is a public community. You can join conversations and access the public community areas without purchasing anything.

Is this only for VORP developers?

No. RedM server owners, developers, players, roleplayers and content creators are welcome. Some technical resources are VORP-focused.

Can I share my own server?

Yes, use the community's relevant showcase channels and follow the server rules. Keep promotion relevant and respectful.

Where do I get support for a Hypas product?

Start with the product documentation and the support information available in Discord. Entitled support depends on your specific licence.
